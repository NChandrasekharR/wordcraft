Signal vs. noise: this runs your current settings once and re-runs a neutral baseline — so you can see how much of the change is your edit versus the model's randomness. Baselines are cached across runs to build a real noise distribution, and a cheap judge scores how much the meaning moved, not just the wording (2–3 generations + 1–2 cheap judge calls per run).
